# names.json — derived from OpenNGC

`names.json` is generated from the OpenNGC database
(<https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC>, © Mattia Verga and contributors), which is
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(CC BY-SA 4.0, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>).

The derived table keeps only object designations and common names (Messier and Caldwell
numbers, Collinder/Melotte/Trumpler/Sharpless/LBN/LDN/Barnard and similar identifiers, and
popular names) and is itself distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The rest of AstroCaption is MIT
licensed; the two are independent works packaged together.

Regenerate with `python scripts/build_names_catalog.py` from `backend/`.
